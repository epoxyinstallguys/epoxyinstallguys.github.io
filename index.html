Planning an epoxy garage or basement floor in Massachusetts

New England winters are hard on bare concrete. Road salt, slush and freeze-thaw cycles pit and stain an untreated slab within a few seasons. A properly installed epoxy system seals the surface so salt water and oil wipe off instead of soaking in.

The prep is what makes a coating last. Concrete should be mechanically ground, cracks and spalls repaired, and moisture checked before the first coat goes down. Skipping the grind is the main reason DIY kits peel under hot tires.

Flake floors hide dust and add grip, metallic finishes suit showrooms and basements, and solid colors work well for workshops and commercial spaces. A clear topcoat adds UV and abrasion resistance.
